🩺 Primary Care
Service 01

General Consultation

Your first stop for any health concern — comprehensive primary care for the whole family.

Our General Consultation service is the foundation of MediCare. Whether you're feeling unwell, need a routine checkup, or want advice on managing a chronic condition, our general physicians are here to help. We take time to listen, ask the right questions, and create a plan that fits your life.

What's Included

  • Full medical history review
  • Physical examination and vitals check
  • Diagnosis and treatment planning
  • Prescription management
  • Referrals to specialists when needed
  • Preventive health advice

What to Expect

A typical consultation lasts 20–30 minutes. We'll review your symptoms, examine you, and discuss a plan together. You'll receive a written summary and any prescriptions needed.

⏱️ Duration: 20–30 minutes
💊 Follow-up: Free within 7 days
📅 Availability: Mon–Sat

❤️ Heart Care
Service 02

Cardiology

Advanced heart care with the latest diagnostics and treatments.

Our Cardiology department is equipped to handle everything from routine heart screenings to complex cardiovascular conditions. Led by experienced cardiologists, we focus on prevention, early detection, and personalized treatment plans that protect your heart health.

What's Included

  • ECG and stress testing
  • Echocardiography
  • Blood pressure and cholesterol management
  • Holter monitoring
  • Cardiac risk assessment
  • Preventive cardiology consultations

What to Expect

Your first visit includes a comprehensive cardiac assessment. We may recommend tests on the same day, and you'll leave with a clear understanding of your heart health and next steps.

⏱️ Duration: 30–45 minutes
💊 Tests: Same-day when possible
📅 Availability: Mon, Wed, Fri

Pediatrics

Gentle, expert care for infants, children, and adolescents.

We understand that children need more than medicine — they need warmth, patience, and a friendly face. Our pediatric team creates a comfortable environment where kids feel safe and parents feel supported. From newborn checkups to teen health, we're here at every stage.

What's Included

  • Vaccinations and immunizations
  • Acute illness treatment
  • Developmental assessments
  • Nutrition and lifestyle guidance
  • Adolescent health support

What to Expect

We allow extra time for pediatric visits so your child never feels rushed. Parents are welcome to stay throughout the appointment, and we encourage questions.

⏱️ Duration: 25–40 minutes
💊 Vaccines: On-site

Complete dental health — from routine cleanings to cosmetic dentistry.

Your smile matters. Our dental team combines modern technology with a gentle approach to keep your teeth and gums healthy. Whether you need a routine cleaning, a filling, or a complete smile makeover, we're equipped to help.

Compassionate support for emotional and psychological well-being.

Mental health is just as important as physical health. Our mental wellness team provides a safe, judgment-free space where you can talk openly about what you're going through. We offer evidence-based therapy and personalized care for a wide range of concerns.

🔬 Diagnostics
Service 06

Diagnostic Services

Accurate testing and imaging for confident diagnosis.

Early and accurate diagnosis is the key to effective treatment. Our diagnostic center uses modern equipment and experienced technicians to deliver reliable results quickly — so you and your doctor can make informed decisions without delay.

What's Included

  • Blood tests and pathology
  • X-rays and ultrasound
  • CT scans and MRI (referral)
  • ECG and cardiac testing
  • Health screening packages
  • Home sample collection (select areas)

What to Expect

Most tests take 15–30 minutes. Results are typically ready within 24–48 hours, and we send them securely to you and your doctor.

⏱️ Duration: 15–30 minutes
📄 Results: Within 24–48 hours
📅 Availability: Mon–Sat

Service 07

Psychiatry

Specialized psychiatric care for complex mental health conditions.

Our Psychiatry department provides advanced diagnosis and treatment for a full range of mental health conditions, including mood disorders, anxiety disorders, trauma-related conditions, and more. Led by board-certified psychiatrists, we combine medication management with therapy and lifestyle support to help you feel your best.

What's Included

  • Comprehensive psychiatric evaluation
  • Medication management
  • Mood and anxiety disorder treatment
  • Trauma and PTSD care
  • OCD and related conditions
  • Telepsychiatry (video consultations)

What to Expect

Your first appointment is a full 60-minute evaluation. We'll discuss your history, symptoms, and goals, then create a personalized treatment plan. Follow-ups are typically 20–30 minutes.

⏱️ Duration: 60 minutes (initial)
🔒 Privacy: Fully confidential
📅 Availability: Mon, Tue, Thu, Fri

🦴 Orthopedics
Service 08

Orthopedics

Expert care for bones, joints, muscles, and everything that keeps you moving.

Whether you're dealing with a sports injury, chronic joint pain, or need joint replacement surgery, our orthopedic team is here to help you get back to doing what you love. We combine surgical expertise with rehabilitation to ensure the best possible recovery.

What's Included

  • Joint pain and arthritis care
  • Sports injury treatment
  • Fracture and trauma care
  • Joint replacement surgery
  • Arthroscopy (minimally invasive)
  • Physiotherapy and rehabilitation

What to Expect

Your first visit includes a full musculoskeletal exam. We may order X-rays or MRIs on the same day. Most patients receive a treatment plan within 48 hours.

⏱️ Duration: 30–45 minutes
🩻 Imaging: On-site
📅 Availability: Mon, Tue, Fri

Service 09

Ophthalmology

Complete eye care — from routine vision tests to advanced surgical treatment.

Your eyes deserve expert attention. Our ophthalmology team provides comprehensive eye exams, vision correction, and treatment for eye conditions affecting patients of all ages. From cataract surgery to pediatric eye care, we're equipped with the latest technology to protect your vision.

What's Included

  • Comprehensive eye exams
  • Prescription glasses and contact lens fitting

  • LASIK and refractive surgery
  • Glaucoma screening and management
