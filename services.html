🩺 Primary Care
Service 01

General Consultation

Your first stop for any health concern — comprehensive primary care for the whole family.

Our General Consultation service is the foundation of MediCare. Whether you're feeling unwell, need a routine checkup, or want advice on managing a chronic condition, our general physicians are here to help.

What's Included

  • Full medical history review
  • Physical examination and vitals check
  • Diagnosis and treatment planning
  • Prescription management
  • Referrals to specialists when needed
  • Preventive health advice

What to Expect

A typical consultation lasts 20–30 minutes. We'll review your symptoms, examine you, and discuss a plan together.

⏱️ Duration: 20–30 minutes
💊 Follow-up: Free within 7 days
📅 Availability: Mon–Sat

Service 02

Cardiology

Advanced heart care with the latest diagnostics and treatments.

Our Cardiology department handles everything from routine heart screenings to complex cardiovascular conditions. We focus on prevention, early detection, and personalized treatment plans.

What's Included

  • ECG and stress testing
  • Echocardiography
  • Blood pressure and cholesterol management
  • Holter monitoring
  • Cardiac risk assessment
  • Preventive cardiology consultations

What to Expect

Your first visit includes a comprehensive cardiac assessment. We may recommend tests on the same day.

⏱️ Duration: 30–45 minutes
💊 Tests: Same-day when possible
📅 Availability: Mon, Wed, Fri

Mental Wellness

Compassionate support for emotional and psychological well-being.

Mental health is just as important as physical health. Our team provides a safe, judgment-free space where you can talk openly about what you're going through.

What's Included

  • Anxiety and depression support
  • Stress management programs

🔬 Diagnostics
Service 06

Diagnostic Services

Accurate testing and imaging for confident diagnosis.

Our diagnostic center uses modern equipment and experienced technicians to deliver reliable results quickly — so you and your doctor can make informed decisions without delay.

What's Included

  • Blood tests and pathology
  • X-rays and ultrasound
  • CT scans and MRI (referral)
  • ECG and cardiac testing
  • Health screening packages
  • Home sample collection (select areas)

What to Expect

Most tests take 15–30 minutes. Results are typically ready within 24–48 hours.

⏱️ Duration: 15–30 minutes
📄 Results: Within 24–48 hours
📅 Availability: Mon–Sat

Psychiatry

Specialized psychiatric care for complex mental health conditions.

Our Psychiatry department provides advanced diagnosis and treatment for a full range of mental health conditions, including mood disorders, anxiety disorders, and trauma-related conditions.

What's Included

  • Comprehensive psychiatric evaluation
  • Medication management
  • Mood and anxiety disorder treatment
  • Trauma and PTSD care
  • OCD and related conditions

Expert care for bones, joints, muscles, and everything that keeps you moving.
